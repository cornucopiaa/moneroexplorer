FAQs about Monero Blockchain Explorer

Can I find out who owns a Monero address?

No, Monero’s privacy design prevents direct ownership identification.

How often is blockchain data updated?

Blockchain data is updated in real-time as new blocks are confirmed.

Is Monero’s price highly volatile?

Yes, like other cryptocurrencies, XMR’s price experiences significant fluctuations.
